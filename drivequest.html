Team project

DriveQuest

Learn. Practice. Drive safer. An interactive app that turns driving education into something people actually want to use.

🚗Learn. Practice. Drive safer.Drive
Quest

The problem

Many new drivers don't have much experience or awareness yet, and that can lead to dangerous situations. A paper handbook is easy to ignore.

What we built

DriveQuest is an interactive learning app for new and teenage drivers. Instead of reading rules, users learn them through an engaging experience, so road safety sticks. The goal is simple: fewer accidents through better decisions behind the wheel.

My role

I handled research and game design. That meant figuring out what new drivers need to know, and shaping how learning it could feel more like playing than studying.
