A remix inspired by “123 Andrés,” arranged with layered vocals, percussion, bass, and synth. The listener chooses an upbeat or chill direction that changes the tempo and mix.

About the project

This EarSketch project builds a track in sections using sampled vocals and instruments. A mood choice sets the tempo and track levels, and the arrangement adds extra sounds as it develops.

What I practiced

  • Arranging audio clips into musical sections with Python.
  • Creating reusable functions for repeated patterns.
  • Changing tempo and effects to give the song a different feel.
