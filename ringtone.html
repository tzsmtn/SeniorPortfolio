Ringtone

A short original ringtone composed in EarSketch, layering drums, bass, synth, and kalimba before ending with a gentle fade.

SOUND DESIGNRING
TONE

About the project

The track is assembled from short sections, with separate functions for the main arrangement and transitions. The closing kalimba line fades out to give the ringtone a clear ending.

What I practiced

  • Layering multiple instruments across tracks.
  • Structuring a composition with reusable functions.
  • Using volume effects to create transitions and a fade-out.
